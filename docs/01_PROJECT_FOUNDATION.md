# 01 — Project Foundation

*What we are building, for whom, why, and what we are not building.*

---

## 1. Executive summary

The Western Uganda Film Producers Association Ltd (WUFPA) is a non-profit company limited
by guarantee, registered in Uganda on 4 September 2017, headquartered in Mbarara City. It
began as 14 production companies from Ankole and Kigezi. It is now 300+ film production
houses across the six sub-regions of Western Uganda `[P1]`.

WUFPA is not a film studio and not a charity. It is a **trade association** — the
organising body for an industry that, until 2017, had no representation outside Kampala.
Its work is infrastructural: training filmmakers, defending their copyright, opening
distribution channels, financing their productions through a SACCO, and putting Western
Ugandan filmmakers in rooms where national policy is decided.

WUFPA currently has no website. It has a 24-page PDF profile, a Facebook and WhatsApp
presence, and a partially-built HTML prototype. Every institution that considers funding,
partnering with or accrediting WUFPA — the Uganda Communications Commission, the US
Mission, UNESCO's national commission, Alliance Française, the Goethe-Zentrum, festival
selectors in Zanzibar and Kigali — currently has to be sent a file to learn who WUFPA is.
Every filmmaker in Kasese or Kagadi who hears the name has nowhere to go to find out how
to join.

**The website's job is to replace that PDF as WUFPA's front door, and to be a better front
door than a PDF can be: current, searchable, linkable, and open at 2am on a phone.**

This is achievable. WUFPA has an unusually strong evidence base for an organisation of its
size — a documented eight-year activity record, a named governance structure of roughly 60
office-holders across four committees and ten craft guilds, photographic documentation of
almost every programme it has run, and institutional partnerships that are visible in
those photographs rather than merely claimed. The task is not to make WUFPA look credible.
It is to stop hiding evidence that is already there.

## 5. Target audiences

Five audiences, in priority order. Priority matters because when two audiences want
different things from the same page, the higher-priority one wins.

### A1 — The prospective member *(primary)*

A filmmaker, actor, editor, sound recordist or small production company somewhere in
Western Uganda. Possibly already making films and selling them informally. Likely on a
mid-range Android phone, likely arriving from a Facebook or WhatsApp link, likely on
metered data.

- **Wants to know:** Is this real? Is it for people like me or only for big companies in
  Mbarara? What do I actually get? What does it cost? Who do I talk to near me?
- **Fears:** paying money to something that turns out to be nothing; being told they are
  not professional enough.
- **Needs to see:** people who look like them, in places they recognise, doing things they
  want to do. Named coordinators in their own district. A clear, low-friction first step.
- **Design implication:** the join path must be obvious from every page, the photography
  must be regional and specific, and the site must be fast and light.

### A2 — The institutional partner or funder *(primary)*

A programme officer at UCC, an embassy cultural attaché, an NGO grants manager, a
foundation, a corporate CSR lead, a festival programmer.

- **Wants to know:** Is this organisation legally constituted, governed, and still active?
  Who is accountable? What have they actually delivered? Can they absorb and account for
  funds?
- **Fears:** wasting time on a body that is one person with a logo; reputational risk.
- **Needs to see:** legal status, named governance with roles, a dated activity record,
  existing partners, and evidence rather than adjectives.
- **Design implication:** legal status, governance and activity record must be reachable in
  one click from the homepage, and must be specific and dated. This audience is the reason
  the site cannot look like a template.

### A3 — The existing member *(secondary, growing)*

Already joined. Comes back for opportunities, deadlines, training, competition calls, and
proof of their own membership.

- **Wants to know:** What is happening? What can I enter? When? Where? How do I submit?
- **Design implication:** the site needs a genuine News and Events capability from day one,
  and a member-facing area is the strongest phase-two candidate (see
  [19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md](19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md)).

### A4 — The journalist, researcher or student *(secondary)*

Writing about Ugandan cinema, regional creative economies, or WUFPA specifically.

- **Wants:** accurate names and titles, spellable and copyable; dates; downloadable logo
  and photographs; someone to contact.
- **Design implication:** a press/media section, correct name spellings, and a downloadable
  profile. Cheap to build, disproportionate reputational return.

### A5 — The general public and diaspora *(tertiary)*

Someone who watched a film on TV West or YoTV, or a Ugandan abroad. Curious; possibly a
future donor or audience member.

- **Design implication:** do not design for this audience, but do not exclude them. Keep
  language plain and the story human.

| Metric | Target, first 6 months | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Membership enquiries submitted | ≥ 15/month by month 6 | Direct proxy for W1/B1. The single most important number |
| Partnership or funding enquiries | ≥ 2/month | Direct proxy for W2/B2 |
| Enquiries originating outside Mbarara district | ≥ 40% of member enquiries | Tests W5 — whether the site actually reaches all six sub-regions or just the head office's own city |

### Tier 2 — Behaviour metrics (diagnostic)

| Metric | Target | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage → any second page | ≥ 55% | If below, the homepage is not answering "is this real?" |
| Median scroll depth, Membership page | ≥ 75% | If below, the membership offer is not compelling or is too long |
| Programmes/Impact pages: median time on page | ≥ 60s | This is the evidence content; skimming means it is not credible or not scannable |
| Mobile share of sessions | Expect 75–90% | Not a target, a constraint check. If mobile is dominant and performance slips, that is an emergency |

### Tier 3 — Health metrics (non-negotiable, not "targets")

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Lighthouse Performance, mobile, Slow 4G throttle | ≥ 90 |
| Largest Contentful Paint, Slow 4G | ≤ 2.5s |
| Total initial page weight, homepage | ≤ 1.2 MB |
| JavaScript shipped, homepage | ≤ 100 KB compressed |
| axe-core critical + serious violations | 0 |
| WCAG 2.2 AA conformance | Full, verified manually — see [17_QA_CHECKLIST.md](17_QA_CHECKLIST.md) |
| Broken links | 0 |

> **On vanity metrics.** Page views, bounce rate and social followers are not success
> metrics for this project and should not be reported as such. WUFPA needs members and
> partners, not traffic. A site with 500 monthly visitors that produces 15 membership
> enquiries has succeeded; one with 50,000 visitors that produces none has failed.

## 7. Constraints

These are real conditions of this project. They are not excuses and they are not
negotiable away — they are inputs that should visibly shape the design.

### C1 — Network and device conditions *(hard constraint)*

The primary audience uses mid-range Android devices on mobile data that is metered and
often billed per megabyte. A heavy site is not merely slow here; it is expensive for the
user, and it excludes exactly the rural and semi-urban creatives WUFPA exists to reach
`[P3]`.

**Consequences:** aggressive image optimisation is mandatory, not an enhancement; JavaScript
is a cost that must be justified per kilobyte; no autoplaying video; no web fonts loaded
before content is readable; the site must be legible and navigable before JavaScript runs.

### C2 — Content supply is the bottleneck, not engineering

Everything the site can say is currently contained in one PDF. There is no film catalogue,
no member register, no news archive, no video, and no event calendar in machine-readable
form. See § 10 for the full list.

**Consequences:** the architecture must be honest about what exists on day one, and must be
built to absorb content later without redesign. Sections that would be empty at launch are
either deferred or designed for a genuine first-item state — never filled with invented
content, and never shipped as an empty shell.

### C3 — No backend, no budget assumption for one

WUFPA has no IT staff, no server, and no evidence of a hosting budget. Anything requiring
a database, authentication or ongoing server maintenance is a liability, not a feature.

**Consequences:** static-first architecture; forms handled by the hosting platform; a
member portal is explicitly a future phase with its own funding conversation, not a launch
feature.

### C4 — Editorial capacity is unknown and probably thin

We have no evidence that WUFPA has a person whose job is to update a website. A CMS nobody
uses is worse than no CMS, because the site then visibly rots.

**Consequences:** the launch site must be true for a long time without edits. Content that
expires — "upcoming event", "this year's competition" — must be structurally contained so
that when it goes stale it degrades gracefully rather than making the whole site look
abandoned. Training and a maintenance plan are deliverables, not afterthoughts.

### C5 — Personal data in the source material

Pages 6–7 of the profile PDF contain the names, roles, districts **and personal mobile
telephone numbers** of approximately 60 individuals. Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy
Act, 2019 applies. Publishing personal mobile numbers of volunteers and office-holders on a
public website exposes them to spam, fraud and harassment, and exposes WUFPA to liability.

**Consequences:** individual mobile numbers are **not** reproduced in this documentation
set and must **not** be published on the website. Names, roles, districts and sub-regions
are governance information and may be published with consent. Contact must route through
association channels. This is expanded in
[11_TEAM_AND_LEADERSHIP.md § 6](11_TEAM_AND_LEADERSHIP.md#6-privacy-and-personal-data).

### C6 — Name spellings and dates in the source are inconsistent

The source document contains internal contradictions in names, dates and roles (catalogued
in [02_ORGANISATION_PROFILE.md § 12](02_ORGANISATION_PROFILE.md#12-conflicts-gaps-and-corrections-register)).
Publishing a person's name wrongly is a real harm to a real person.

**Consequences:** no name goes live without WUFPA confirming its spelling. This is a launch
blocker, not a nice-to-have.

### C7 — Language

All supplied material is in English. Western Uganda's audiences speak Runyankole-Rukiga,
Rutooro-Runyoro, Rukonzo and others, and WUFPA's own mission is built on local-language
storytelling `[P3]`. There is no supplied translated content and no stated language policy.

**Consequences:** English at launch, but the architecture must not preclude localisation.
See [19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md](19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md).

## 8. Project scope

### 8.1 In scope — Release 1.0

**Pages** (full sitemap in [05_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md](05_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md)):
Home · About (incl. legal status, vision, mission, objectives, history) · Leadership &
Governance · Programmes (index + individual programme pages) · Regions (index + six
sub-region pages) · Guilds · Membership (incl. how to join) · WUFM SACCO · Partners ·
Impact/Gallery · News (index + articles) · Events (index + event pages) · Contact ·
Support WUFPA · Legal (privacy, terms, accessibility statement).

**Capabilities:** membership enquiry form · partnership/general contact form · news and
events as structured content · photo gallery with real captions · Google Maps embed for
the head office · social links · site search · full SEO and structured data ·
WCAG 2.2 AA conformance · analytics.

**Assets:** processing, renaming, optimising and captioning the supplied photographic
archive; extracting leadership portraits and activity photographs from the profile PDF;
producing responsive derivatives of the logo including an SVG redraw.

### 8.2 Out of scope — Release 1.0

Each exclusion has a reason. "We ran out of time" is not one of them.

| Excluded | Why |
|---|---|
| Member login / member portal | Requires authentication, a member database WUFPA does not have in digital form, and ongoing administration (C3, C4). High value, so it is the first item in the future roadmap — but it is a project, not a feature |
| Online membership payment | No supplied fee structure, no payment provider decision, no reconciliation process. `⚠ GAP` |
| Film catalogue / streaming / VOD | No film data supplied at all — no titles, no stills, no rights information. Nothing to build with |
| Online festival submission system | The UCC competition has its own process; duplicating it would confuse entrants |
| E-commerce / merchandise | No product, no fulfilment capability |
| Multi-language | C7. No translated content exists |
| Blog with a publishing cadence | We cannot commit WUFPA to a cadence it has not agreed to. News is built; frequency is WUFPA's call |
| Donations processing | `⚠ GAP` — no payment channel supplied. The prototype's Donate button links to `#`. Release 1.0 presents supported ways to help with a real contact route, and adds payment when WUFPA supplies a verified channel |

### 8.3 Explicitly excluded on principle

- **Stock photography of people.** See § 9.
- **Invented organisational facts** of any kind.
- **Individual personal phone numbers** (C5).
- **A cookie banner**, unless analytics that require consent are chosen. The preferred
  analytics approach is cookieless — see [18_DEPLOYMENT_AND_MAINTENANCE.md](18_DEPLOYMENT_AND_MAINTENANCE.md).

| **X35** | All imagery is hot-linked from a third-party CDN (`images.unsplash.com`) with no local fallback. WUFPA's site would break if that service changed terms — and the images are not WUFPA's to publish |
| **X36** | `lang="en"` with no region; no `hreflang` strategy |
| **X37** | Section IDs are used for navigation on a single page. Everything is one URL, so nothing is separately linkable, shareable or indexable — which defeats goal W4 |

### 9.6 Verdict

**Rebuild, retaining the strategy.** The prototype's information sequence is sound and is
being carried forward with credit. Its implementation cannot be incrementally fixed,
because the two largest problems — fabricated content and stock imagery of people — are not
bugs in the code but a different relationship to the truth than this project can have. The
correct action is to rebuild on the architecture in
[14_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md](14_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md), reusing the thinking
documented in § 9.1.

## 11. Future vision

Where this is heading, so that Release 1.0 is built as a foundation rather than a
destination. Detail in [19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md](19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md).

**Year 1 — the public record.** The site becomes the reliable, current, citable account of
who WUFPA is and what it does. Success is that WUFPA stops emailing a 28 MB PDF.

**Year 2 — the member's tool.** A member area: digital membership cards, the register,
opportunity and deadline feeds, resource downloads, and guild-specific spaces. This is
where the ten craft guilds `[P7]` become genuinely useful rather than a governance
diagram.

**Year 3 — the regional industry platform.** A searchable database of Western Ugandan films
and crews — the thing that makes a producer in Kampala or Kigali able to find a
cinematographer in Fort Portal. Plus festival and awards management, a training portal,
and a funding-opportunities board.

The architectural implication for Release 1.0 is stated once and applies throughout:
**model the content, not the page.** Members, films, programmes, events, regions, guilds
and people are entities with relationships. If they are modelled as entities from day one —
even when the store is a handful of markdown files — every future phase is an addition. If
they are hand-written into page templates, every future phase is a rebuild.
