# 10 — Membership

*The primary business goal of the website. What membership is, how someone joins, and how
the site converts interest into an application.*

---

## 1. Why this is the most important section of the site

Membership is business goal B1 and website goal W1
([01 § 3–4](01_PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md#3-business-goals)). It is also the mechanism behind
every other goal: WUFPA's leverage with UCC, broadcasters, embassies and Parliament comes
from representing 300+ production houses rather than four. Growth in membership is growth in
negotiating power.

It follows that **the membership path is the one journey that may never be compromised for
any other consideration.** If a design decision makes the homepage more elegant and the join
path less obvious, the design decision loses.

---

## 2. What membership is — the sourced position

### 2.1 Who WUFPA brings together `[P2]`

- Independent filmmakers and film production companies
- Community-based film groups
- Regional film guilds and creative collectives
- Youth, women and professionals involved in film and visual storytelling
- Members spanning the entire Western Uganda region — Ankole (Rwizi), Kigezi, Rwenzori,
  Tooro, Bunyoro and Greater Bushenyi

Note how broad this is: it explicitly includes individuals, not only registered companies.
That matters, because the biggest barrier for a prospective member is likely to be the
assumption that WUFPA is only for established production houses. **The site should say
plainly that it is not.**

### 2.2 Scale

| Figure | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Founding members | 14 companies/groups | Sourced `[P1] [P4] [P5]` |
| Production houses now | 300+ | Sourced `[P1]`, `⚠ VERIFY` currency (Q16) |
| Members per production house | over 35 | Sourced `[P1]` |
| Individual creatives | ~10,000 | **Derived**, must be labelled an estimate (X13) |

### 2.3 Two membership units

The source consistently describes membership in two units that the site must not conflate:

- **Production houses / companies / groups** — the 300+ figure `[P1]`
- **Individuals within them** — the "over 35 members" each `[P1]`

Whether an individual joins WUFPA directly or through a production house is **not
documented**. This is part of question Q8 and it materially affects the join form. Do not
assume.

### 3.1 How to present benefits

**Group them by the problem they solve, not by WUFPA's internal structure.** A prospective
member is not thinking "capacity building"; they are thinking "I need to learn, I need money,
I need people to see my film".

| Framing on the page | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Learn your craft** | Training, master classes, mentorship, guilds |
| **Get your film seen** | Distribution, broadcast, Kibanda, competitions, festivals |
| **Get your film financed** | WUFM SACCO |
| **Protect your work** | Copyright advocacy, anti-piracy, rights |
| **Have a voice** | Representation, guilds, sub-region coordination |

Each item links to the programme page that proves it. **Evidence, not adjectives**
([03 § 2.1](03_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md#21-the-voice-in-four-rules)).

### 3.2 The certificate is worth emphasising

Four separate photographs show WUFPA presenting membership and merit certificates
`[PH-1] [PH-4] [P5] [P7]`. People are visibly pleased to be holding them.

For a filmmaker operating informally, a certificate from a URSB-registered association is a
tangible credential — usable when approaching a broadcaster, a bank, or a district office.
This is a real and specific benefit that the source material evidences and that the prototype
missed entirely. It should be shown, with the photographs.

## 4. The membership journey

### 4.1 The journey as it must work

| Stage | Where | The visitor's question | What must happen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Encounter** | Facebook/WhatsApp link → `/` | "What is this?" | Real photograph, plain headline, region named |
| **2. Recognise** | `/` scroll, `/regions/` | "Is this for people like me?" | See their district. See people who look like them |
| **3. Understand** | `/membership/` | "What do I get?" | Benefits framed by problem (§ 3.1), each evidenced |
| **4. Qualify** | `/membership/` | "Am I eligible? What does it cost?" | ⟦Q8⟧ — currently unanswerable |
| **5. Apply** | `/membership/join/` | "What do I have to do?" | Short form, ≤ 2 minutes on a phone |
| **6. Confirm** | Success state | "Did that work? What now?" | Explicit next step and who will contact them |
| **7. Follow-up** | Off-site | "When will I hear?" | WUFPA's process — outside the website's control |

### 4.2 The three friction points, and the fix for each

**Friction 1 — "This looks like it's for big companies in Mbarara."**
*Fix:* the eligibility list appears high on `/membership/`, in plain language, explicitly
including individuals and community groups `[P2]`. Regional photography, not head-office
photography. Districts named.

**Friction 2 — "I don't know what it costs, so I'll assume I can't afford it."**
*Fix:* state the cost plainly, whatever it is. ⟦Q8 — blocking.⟧ An unstated fee is read as an
expensive fee. If membership is free, saying so is the single highest-conversion sentence on
the site; the prototype asserted this `[SITE]` but no source supports it, so it cannot be
published until confirmed.

**Friction 3 — "I filled in a form once and nobody replied."**
*Fix:* the success message names the next step and who takes it. Where possible, name the
sub-region coordinator who will make contact — this converts an anonymous form into a
person-to-person handoff, which is how this audience actually operates.

### 4.3 Journey design consequences

- **"Become a Member" appears in the header on every page**, and in the mobile menu pinned
  above the fold.
- **`/membership/` and `/membership/join/` are separate pages.** The first persuades, the
  second converts. Merging them produces a page that is too long to read and too long to
  fill in.
- **Every programme page ends with a membership CTA**, because a visitor who has just read
  the training page is at the highest point of intent they will reach.
- **Every region page ends with "Join WUFPA in {Region}"** — localised intent converts better
  than generic intent.

## 5. The membership application form

### 5.1 Design principles

1. **Every field must earn its place.** Each additional field costs completions. WUFPA needs
   enough to make contact and route the enquiry — not a full membership record. The rest is
   collected by the coordinator, in conversation.
2. **Mobile-first, single column, ≤ 2 minutes.** The dominant device is a mid-range Android
   phone on metered data (constraint C1).
3. **No account, no login, no file upload at launch.** Uploads fail on poor connections and
   require storage and moderation WUFPA does not have.
4. **Works without JavaScript.** Native form POST to the hosting platform's form handler
   ([13_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md](13_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md)).

### 5.2 Specified fields

| # | Field | Type | Required | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your name | text | ✔ | Contact |
| 2 | Phone number (WhatsApp preferred) | tel | ✔ | **The primary contact channel for this audience.** WhatsApp is one of WUFPA's two stated channels `[P3]` |
| 3 | Email address | email | ✖ | Many members will not have one. Making it required would exclude them |
| 4 | Sub-region | select (6) | ✔ | Routes the enquiry to the right coordinator `[P6] [P7]` |
| 5 | District | text | ✔ | Routes to the district coordinator |
| 6 | I am applying as | radio: An individual creative / A production company or group / A film guild or collective | ✔ | Matches the documented membership categories `[P2]` |
| 7 | Name of your company or group | text | conditional | Only shown for the second and third options |
| 8 | What do you do in film? | checkbox group, the ten guild crafts + "Other" | ✔ | Routes to the right guild `[P7]`; also the most useful single piece of data WUFPA could gather |
| 9 | Tell us about your work | textarea | ✖ | Optional, open. Gives the coordinator something to open a conversation with |
| 10 | *(honeypot)* | hidden | — | Spam |
| 11 | Consent | checkbox | ✔ | See § 7 |

**Eleven fields, of which seven are required and two are conditional.** Field 8 is a
checkbox group rather than a select because filmmakers in this context routinely do several
jobs.

### 5.3 What is deliberately not asked

| Not asked | Why |
|---|---|
| Postal address | Not needed to make contact; a barrier in areas without addressing |
| Date of birth / age | Not needed; adds a data-protection obligation for no benefit |
| Gender | WUFPA's funding case references youth and women `[P3]`, so there may be a real reporting need — but it must be optional, self-described, and justified. Recommend collecting it later in the process, not at first contact |
| National ID / passport number | Never on a public web form. High-value personal data with no launch use case |
| Company registration number | Excludes informal groups, who are explicitly welcome `[P2]` |
| Showreel or film upload | Bandwidth and moderation cost; belongs in a member portal ([19](19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md)) |
| Payment | ⟦Q8⟧ — and payment at the enquiry stage would be wrong regardless. Application first, payment after acceptance |

### 5.4 Form behaviour

- Validation on blur and submit; never on keystroke.
- On failure: an error summary at the top listing each error as a link to its field; focus
  moves there ([08 § 37](08_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md#37-form)).
- On success: focus moves to the confirmation, which is announced.
- Submission routes to WUFPA's designated address ⟦Q3⟧ with the sub-region in the subject
  line so it can be forwarded to the right coordinator.
- Honeypot only; **no CAPTCHA** — an accessibility barrier and a conversion tax at WUFPA's
  volume.

### 5.5 Confirmation copy

> **Thank you. Your details have reached WUFPA.**
>
> A coordinator in ⟦sub-region⟧ will contact you on the number you gave us.
>
> ⟦Q8: expected timeframe⟧
>
> While you wait: read about **what we do** → `/programmes/`, or find **your sub-region** →
> `/regions/`.

The two onward links matter. A confirmation page that is a dead end wastes the moment of
highest engagement in the entire journey.

## 6. "Become a Member" — CTA strategy

| Placement | Variant | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Header, every page | Primary button | Always one click from intent |
| Mobile menu, pinned | Full-width primary | Reachable without scrolling the menu |
| Homepage hero | Primary, paired with one secondary | One primary action, not three (X8) |
| Homepage membership band | Primary, with the offer summarised | The natural conversion point |
| Every programme page foot | Primary | Peak intent |
| Every region page foot | Primary, localised: "Join WUFPA in {Region}" | Localised intent converts better |
| Every guild page foot | Primary, localised: "Join the {Guild}" | Same principle, craft axis |
| SACCO page | Secondary — the SACCO is a reason to join, and the primary action there is a SACCO enquiry | Do not muddle two different asks |
| News articles | Contextual, not automatic | An article about a partner meeting does not need a membership CTA |

**Label discipline:** the label is always "Become a member" or a localised variant. Not
"Join us", not "Sign up", not "Get involved". One label, learned once, recognised everywhere.

## 7. Data protection and consent

Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 applies to the personal data this form
collects. This is not optional compliance theatre — WUFPA is collecting names and phone
numbers of members of the public.

**Requirements at launch:**

1. **A consent checkbox**, unticked by default, with specific wording:
   > "I agree that WUFPA may store these details and contact me about membership."
2. **A privacy notice** at `/legal/privacy/` stating what is collected, why, who sees it, how
   long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.
3. **A named route for deletion requests** ⟦Q3⟧.
4. **No third-party analytics or marketing tags on the form pages** beyond the cookieless
   analytics chosen in [18_DEPLOYMENT_AND_MAINTENANCE.md](18_DEPLOYMENT_AND_MAINTENANCE.md).
5. **Submissions are emailed, not stored in a database**, at launch. Less data at rest, less
   risk, and nothing for WUFPA to administer.

> This same statute is the reason the ~60 personal mobile numbers in the source document are
> not published anywhere on the site
> ([01 § 7 C5](01_PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md#7-constraints)). The obligation runs in both
> directions: WUFPA protects its members' data and its office-holders' data alike.

## 8. Our members — the directory question

`⚠ GAP` Q16. Only four member companies are documented `[P4] [P5]`.

**Decision: no member directory in Release 1.0.**

The reasoning is worth recording, because it will be questioned. A page headed "Our 300+
members" listing four companies actively damages the claim it is meant to support — a
sceptical funder reads it as evidence that the 300 figure is soft. The prototype's solution
was to invent four additional companies and add a "+292 More Production Houses" card (X3),
which is the failure this decision prevents.

**What `/membership/` shows instead:**

> WUFPA is made up of more than 300 film production houses across Western Uganda. `[P1]`
>
> **Four of our member production companies:**
> Ankole City Filmz (Mbarara) · Kigezi Universal Drama Actors/Artist (Kabale) ·
> Banyankitara Films · UPSKY Film Network

Explicitly labelled as four examples, not a directory. Locations for the last two are
unconfirmed ([02 § 12.1 C-2, C-3](02_ORGANISATION_PROFILE.md#121-conflicts-between-sources)).

**When a directory becomes possible:** WUFPA supplies a register with, at minimum, company
name, sub-region, district and craft. At that point it becomes a filterable directory — the
single most valuable addition to the site, because it turns membership from a claim into an
inspectable fact, and simultaneously becomes a discovery tool for anyone looking to hire crew
in Western Uganda.

## 9. Future member portal

Full treatment: [19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md](19_FUTURE_ROADMAP.md). Recorded here because the
launch build must not preclude it.

**What it would do, in priority order:**

1. **Digital membership card** — verifiable proof of membership, with a public verification
   URL. Directly extends the certificate benefit (§ 3.2) and is genuinely useful when
   approaching a broadcaster or a bank.
2. **The member register** — searchable by craft, region and district. Members find each
   other; that is what a guild structure is *for*.
3. **Opportunities and deadlines** — competition calls, training places, delegation
   selection, funding.
4. **Resource library** — contracts, release forms, rate guidance, copyright templates.
   Practical, and it directly serves the anti-piracy mission.
5. **Guild spaces** — each of the ten guilds with its own area.
6. **Renewal and payment** — once a fee structure exists (Q8).

**What Release 1.0 must do to keep this cheap later:**

- Model members, guilds, regions and people as related entities from day one
  ([14 § 5](14_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md)) — even with four members in the collection.
- Reserve `/membership/members/`, `/members/[slug]/` and `/portal/` in the route plan
  ([05 § 8](05_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md#8-future-expansion)).
- Capture craft and sub-region on the application form (§ 5.2, fields 4, 5 and 8) so that
  when a register is built, the data shape already matches.

**What it must not do:** ship a login form that does nothing, or a "Member Area — Coming
Soon" link. An unfinished portal signals an unfinished organisation
([08 § 34](08_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md#34-emptystate)).

## 10. Membership questions for WUFPA

Consolidating the blocking items. Q8 in
[01 § 10](01_PROJECT_FOUNDATION.md#10-open-questions-for-wufpa) is really eight questions:

| # | Question | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| 8a | Is there a membership fee? How much, and how often? | The whole `/membership/` cost section |
| 8b | Is the fee per production house, per individual, or both? | Form field 6 and the pricing display |
| 8c | Can an individual join directly, or only through a production house? | Form field 6, eligibility copy |
| 8d | What are the eligibility criteria, if any? | Eligibility copy |
| 8e | What are the steps from application to membership? | "How to join" section |
| 8f | How long does it take? | Confirmation copy |
| 8g | Who contacts the applicant — the sub-region coordinator, the Membership and Welfare Officer, or head office? | Confirmation copy and form routing |
| 8h | Is the membership certificate `[PH-1] [PH-4]` issued to all members, or is it a merit award? | Benefits section — these are photographed as "Certificate of Merit", which may be a different thing from a membership certificate |

Question 8h matters more than it looks: the certificates visible in `[PH-1]` read "Certificate
of Merit", which suggests recognition rather than membership. Presenting a merit award as a
standard membership benefit would be an invented claim.
