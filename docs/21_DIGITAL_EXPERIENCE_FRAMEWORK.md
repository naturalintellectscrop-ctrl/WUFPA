# 21 — The WUFPA Digital Experience Framework

*How the site should feel, and the craft standard every page is designed to.*

> Supplied by WUFPA, 31 July 2026. Recorded here verbatim in substance so that it governs
> implementation rather than living in a chat log.
>
> **Precedence.** [20_WUFPA_QUALITY_MANIFESTO.md](20_WUFPA_QUALITY_MANIFESTO.md) remains
> supreme: it governs *what is true*. This document governs *how the true thing is
> presented*. Where the two appear to conflict, document 20 wins — but in practice they do
> not, because this framework independently arrives at the same rules ("Never use
> placeholder content", "Never invent statistics or achievements", "Use authentic
> photography"). Where **any other** document (03, 06, 07, 08) conflicts with this one on a

## 1. What this document is for

The objective is not a modern-looking website. The objective is the official digital home
of the Western Uganda Film Producers Association — a site that reflects the
professionalism, creativity, credibility and long-term vision of the organisation and the
film industry it represents.

Every visitor should immediately feel that WUFPA is active, trusted, collaborative and
committed to growing the film industry in Western Uganda.

This framework governs every page, section, interaction, animation, photograph, video,
piece of copy and engineering decision on the site.

---

## 2. The Four-Part Experience Formula

**Every page, section and feature defines these four elements before implementation
begins.** A task card that cannot state all four is not ready to be worked.

### 2.1 Identity — the character of the organisation

Five to eight words. Every design decision must reinforce them.

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | Authentic | Collaborative | Aspirational |
| Cultural | Editorial | Trustworthy | Timeless |

If a page does not communicate these qualities, it is refined until it does.

### 2.2 Reference — borrow quality, never appearance

Every design decision is informed by exceptional digital experiences. Study them for:

typography · layout rhythm · visual hierarchy · photography treatment · editorial
composition · motion quality · white space · navigation clarity · content pacing

**Never copy:** colours · branding · icons · illustrations · industry-specific layouts.

The named quality references are the **Academy Museum** (editorial storytelling), the
**British Film Institute** (institutional credibility), **A24** (cinematic confidence) and
**National Geographic** (documentary authenticity). The goal is to understand
craftsmanship, not to imitate style. The result must be a WUFPA identity, not a blend of
four other identities.

> `⚠ GAP` — **The reference images named in the supplied brief were not attached.** This
> section records the four named institutional references, which were supplied as text.
> If WUFPA intended specific reference imagery to accompany them, it is still outstanding.
> Nothing is blocked by this: the four named references are sufficient direction.

### 2.3 Experience Intent — how visitors should feel

Every page evokes one clear emotional response. **These are acceptance criteria, not
mood-setting**, and are carried into the page tasks in
[IMPLEMENTATION_QUEUE.md](../IMPLEMENTATION_QUEUE.md).

| Page | Visitors should feel / understand |
|---|---|
| **Homepage** | Inspired · Welcome · Curious · Confident |
| **About** | Who WUFPA is · Why it exists · Why it matters |
| **Programmes** | Encouraged · Included · Empowered |
| **Events** | Excited · Connected · Motivated to participate |
| **Membership** | Valued · Represented · Ready to join |
| **Partners** | Confidence · Credibility · Institutional strength |
| **Contact** | Supported · Comfortable reaching out · Connected to a real organisation |

Every visual and written element reinforces the intended emotion.

### 2.4 Design Guardrails — permanent principles

**Always**

- Design with purpose.
- Let content lead the layout.
- Use authentic photography.
- Tell real stories.
- Create generous breathing space.
- Maintain a clear visual hierarchy.
- Keep navigation predictable.
- Use restrained motion.
- Prioritise accessibility.
- Reuse design patterns consistently.
- Make every section contribute to the organisation's story.

**Never**

- Use placeholder content.
- Invent statistics or achievements.
- Use generic AI-generated copy.
- Fill space with decorative components.
- Overuse gradients or visual effects.
- Create unnecessary animations.
- Use oversized cards simply to occupy space.
- Introduce visual clutter.
- Add sections without a clear purpose.
- Make the website feel like a startup landing page.

**These principles override any individual implementation request.** A request to add a
section that fails a guardrail is refused and the reason recorded, not accommodated.

## 3. Website philosophy

This is not a brochure. This is not a portfolio. This is not a corporate template.

This is the digital headquarters of WUFPA. Every page should strengthen trust in the
organisation while celebrating the people and programmes shaping the film industry in
Western Uganda. The site should feel established, active and enduring.

### 3.1 Storytelling first

Every page answers:

- Why does this organisation exist?
- How is it creating impact?
- Who benefits?
- How can visitors become involved?

The narrative focuses on people, programmes, partnerships and progress — not marketing
language. Real stories create credibility.

### 3.2 Information hierarchy

Every page immediately answers: *Where am I? What is this page about? Why is it important?
What should I do next?* Visitors never have to search for the primary message.

### 3.3 Content before decoration

Design amplifies content; it does not compete with it. Images, video, typography and
layout support the story being told. No decorative section that does not communicate
value.

## 4. Components as a unified system

Every reusable element behaves consistently. This section is intent;
[08_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md](08_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md) is the specification.

| Element | The rule |
|---|---|
| **Buttons** | One primary style. One secondary style. One text style |
| **Cards** | Shared spacing, typography, interaction and elevation |
| **Forms** | Consistent validation, spacing and accessibility |
| **Navigation** | Simple, predictable, persistent |
| **Typography** | One scale. One rhythm. One editorial voice |
| **Icons** | One icon family throughout the site |
| **Motion** | One animation language |

---

## 5. Motion philosophy

Motion communicates **continuity, context, navigation and hierarchy**. It is never
decoration. Animations are subtle and purposeful.

*Premium experiences are remembered for their restraint.*

---

## 6. Editorial rhythm

Alternate between narrative · photography · white space · calls to action · supporting
content. This creates a natural reading experience. Visitors should feel guided, not
overwhelmed.

---

## 7. Photography philosophy

**Photography is evidence.** Use authentic images of workshops, festivals, community
outreach, meetings, film productions, training sessions, leadership and members.

Avoid stock photography wherever possible. Authenticity builds trust.

> On this project the "wherever possible" is absolute for images of people: see
> [20 § Principle 3](20_WUFPA_QUALITY_MANIFESTO.md) and working rule R2. No stock
> photograph of a person, anywhere, for any duration.

---

## 8. Video philosophy

Videos document activity: events, film screenings, workshops, interviews, community
engagement. They enrich the story rather than distract from it. Optimise for performance.

---

## 9. Colour philosophy

Colour communicates meaning. Primary colours reinforce the WUFPA brand; neutrals provide
structure; accents draw attention to key actions. **Never use colour purely for
decoration.** Values are in [07 § 2](07_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#2-colour-tokens).

---

## 10. Typography

Typography communicates professionalism and clarity.

- Use **hierarchy** before colour.
- Use **spacing** before borders.
- Use **weight** before effects.

It must support long-form reading as comfortably as short promotional content.

---

## 11. White space

White space communicates confidence. Do not fear generous spacing. Premium institutional
websites feel calm, organised and easy to read.

---

## 12. Responsive experience

Desktop makes full use of available space. Tablet preserves readability. Mobile simplifies
layout while maintaining the same narrative flow.

**Do not simply resize layouts. Recompose them for each device.**

---

## 13. Accessibility

Every visitor must be able to engage with the site: semantic HTML, keyboard navigation,
screen reader support, high contrast, descriptive alt text, accessible forms, motion
reduction where appropriate.

**Accessibility is part of professionalism.** Specification in
[16_SEO_ACCESSIBILITY.md](16_SEO_ACCESSIBILITY.md).

---

## 14. The authenticity checklist

Before approving any page:

- [ ] Does this represent WUFPA honestly?
- [ ] Does this reflect the supplied documentation?
- [ ] Does the photography feel authentic?
- [ ] Is the hierarchy clear?
- [ ] Is there a single dominant message?
- [ ] Does the page communicate trust?
- [ ] Does the page encourage meaningful engagement?
- [ ] Could this page still feel relevant five years from now?

If any answer is "no", the design is refined.

---

## 15. Final principle

Do not optimise for visual complexity. Optimise for clarity, credibility and purpose.

The best institutional websites do not demand attention. They earn trust through
thoughtful storytelling, consistent design, authentic content and careful craftsmanship.

When visitors leave with a stronger understanding of WUFPA, confidence in its mission and
a desire to engage with the organisation, the design has achieved its purpose.

### 16.2 What it adds that the set did not have

1. **A per-page emotional acceptance criterion** (§ 2.3). Documents 05 and 06 state what
   each page must *do*; this states what each page must make a visitor *feel*. Page tasks
   in the queue now carry it.
2. **The four named quality references** (§ 2.2) — Academy Museum, BFI, A24, National
   Geographic. The set previously had no external craft benchmark at all.
3. **"Recompose, don't resize"** (§ 12) — stronger than document 07 § 10, which describes
   responsive behaviour but does not forbid a straight reflow of a desktop composition.
4. **An explicit precedence claim for craft guardrails** over individual implementation
   requests (§ 2.4).

### 16.3 The one genuine conflict

**The framework's own prose is direction to the builder. It is not site copy.**

Its supplied text uses "growing the film ecosystem" and "Aspirational". Both collide with
[03 § 3](03_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md) house style: **"ecosystem"** is on the banned-words list
as consultancy filler that says nothing and dates fast, and "aspirational" language is
governed by voice rule 1, *evidence, not adjectives*.

**Resolution — no change to either document.** "Aspirational" is retained here as an
*identity word describing how the site should feel*; it is delivered by showing what
members achieved, never by an adjective in the copy. The banned-words list in
[03 § 3](03_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md) continues to govern every published word, and
"ecosystem" does not appear on the site. Wherever the phrase is needed in a document, it
is written as *"the film industry in Western Uganda"* — as it is throughout this file.
