## Who we are

This website is published by the **Western Uganda Film Producers Association Limited**
("WUFPA", "we", "us"), a company limited by guarantee registered in Uganda with the Uganda
Registration Services Bureau on 4 September 2017, operating as a not-for-profit association.

Our head office is on Mbaguta Street, Mbarara Shopping Market, Mbarara City, Uganda.

For the purposes of Uganda's **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019**, WUFPA is the data
collector, data processor and data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

## What this notice covers

This notice covers the personal data you give us **through this website**. It does not cover
data you give us in person, by telephone, or through Facebook or WhatsApp, though we handle
that data on the same principles.

## What we collect, and why

We only ask for information we actually need in order to reply to you. We do not ask for
anything we cannot act on.

### If you send us a message through the contact form

| What we collect | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your name | To address you properly when we reply |
| Your email address | This is where our reply goes. Without it we cannot respond |
| Your sub-region *(optional)* | To route your message to the right coordinator |
| What you are writing about | To send your message to the right person |
| Your message | To understand and answer your enquiry |

### If you apply for membership

| What we collect | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your name | To identify your application |
| Your phone number | The main way our coordinators reach members. WhatsApp is widely used across our sub-regions |
| Your email address *(optional)* | An alternative way to reach you. Optional because many of our members do not use email |
| Your sub-region and district | To route your application to the coordinator nearest you |
| Whether you are applying as an individual, a company or group, or a guild | To apply the right membership category |
| The name of your company or group *(if applicable)* | To identify the organisation applying |
| What you do in film | To connect you with the right craft guild |
| Anything you tell us about your work *(optional)* | To give the coordinator who contacts you some context |

We also ask you to tick a box confirming that you agree to us storing these details and
contacting you about membership. **That box is never ticked for you**, and the form cannot be
submitted without it.

### What we deliberately do not ask for

We do not ask for your national identity number, your date of birth, your bank or mobile-money
details, your photograph, or any information about your health, religion, ethnicity or
political views. None of it is needed to process a membership application or answer an
enquiry.

## How your information is handled

When you submit a form on this website, the details are **sent to WUFPA by email**. They are
not stored in a database on this website, and this website has no login, no user accounts and
no member area.

We chose this deliberately. Less data held means less data at risk.

## Who sees your information

Your information is seen by:

- the WUFPA officers who handle enquiries and membership applications; and
- the **sub-regional or district coordinator** for the area you named, so that someone near you
  can follow up.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers, and we do not pass
it to any other organisation for their own marketing.

We may disclose personal data where we are required to do so by Ugandan law, or by an order of
a court or a regulator.

## Cookies and analytics

**This website does not set any cookies**, and it does not track you across other websites.

There are no advertising pixels, no session recording, no heatmaps and no third-party
marketing tags anywhere on this site.

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- **be told** what personal data we hold about you;
- **see** that data;
- **correct** it if it is wrong or incomplete;
- **ask us to delete** it;
- **object** to us using it, including for any direct marketing; and
- **complain** to the Personal Data Protection Office if you believe we have mishandled your
  data.

Exercising any of these rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

## Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from
anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will
delete it.

## Links to other websites

Some pages link to other organisations (partners, funders, festivals and regulators). Those
websites have their own privacy notices, and we are not responsible for how they handle your
data.

## Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this notice and change the date
at the top of this page. Where a change is significant, we will say so.
