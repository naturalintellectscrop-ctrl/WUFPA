## Our commitment

WUFPA is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people who use screen
readers, magnification, keyboard-only navigation, or who browse with images or JavaScript
turned off.

This matters to us for three reasons.

Our members use this site on small phones, in bright daylight, over slow and expensive mobile
data. The work that makes a site accessible (real contrast, large touch targets, keyboard
operability, pages that work without JavaScript) is the same work that makes it usable in
those conditions.

WUFPA also seeks support from UNESCO's national commission, diplomatic missions and
development partners, and accessibility is a stated requirement for many of them.

Most of all, an association whose purpose includes the inclusion of underrepresented people
cannot publish a website that excludes disabled people.

## The standard we are working to

We are working to **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, and we have adopted several Level AAA measures where
they matter for our audience, including a minimum touch target of 44 by 44 pixels, body text
of at least 17 pixels, and plain language, since English is a second or third language for many
of our members.

screen-reader assessment, so we are not yet claiming conformance.

## What we have done

- Every page uses real headings in order, with one main heading per page. This is checked
  automatically on every change, and a page that breaks it cannot be published.
- Every image carries alternative text describing what it shows. Images that are purely
  decorative are marked as such deliberately, rather than by omission.
- A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing a keyboard user reaches on every page.
- Everything can be operated by keyboard, and the keyboard focus indicator is a clear
  three-pixel outline that is visible on every background we use.
- Text contrast is well above the minimum. Our body text sits at about 11:1 against white,
  where the standard asks for 4.5:1.
- Buttons and links are at least 44 by 44 pixels, with space between them.
- Nothing moves, flashes or plays on its own. There is no automatic carousel.
- If you have asked your device to reduce motion, we honour that setting throughout.
- The content is in the page itself, so it works with JavaScript turned off and on a slow
  connection.
- The site can be zoomed to 200% and used on a 320-pixel-wide screen without content being lost
  or requiring sideways scrolling.

## Known limitations

We would rather name these than leave you to discover them.

**This site is still being published.** Several sections described in our plans are not yet
live. This is a limitation of completeness, not of accessibility, but it may be why you cannot
find something.

**We have not yet completed screen-reader testing.** Testing with NVDA, VoiceOver and TalkBack
is planned before launch. Until it is done, we cannot promise that every part of the site reads
sensibly aloud.

**Our downloadable association profile is a PDF.** PDFs are frequently difficult to use with a
screen reader, and this one has not been remediated. If you need any of that information in
another format, ask us and we will provide it.

**We have not published any video.** When we do, it will carry captions. We will not publish
video without them.

## How we tested

Automated accessibility checks run against every change to this website, and they cover the
mechanical parts of the standard: heading order, missing alternative text, missing form
labels, contrast, and so on.

We are conscious that **automated tools find roughly a third of accessibility problems**. They
cannot tell us whether alternative text is actually helpful, whether the focus order makes
sense, or whether a label means anything out of context. The manual testing planned before
launch (keyboard-only navigation, screen readers on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, 200% and
400% zoom, high-contrast mode, and browsing with JavaScript disabled) is where conformance
will actually be established.

⟦WUFPA-075: record the date the manual audit was carried out, and by whom, once it is done.⟧

## Telling us about a problem

If any part of this website does not work for you, we want to know. You do not need to explain
the technical reason, just tell us the page and what happened.

⟦Q3: WUFPA to supply the association email address for accessibility reports, and to commit to
a response time, for example "we will reply within five working days". A statement that
invites reports without giving a route to send them is not a statement, and a response
commitment WUFPA cannot keep is worse than a longer one it can.⟧

You can also reach us at our head office on Mbaguta Street, Mbarara Shopping Market, Mbarara
City, Uganda.

If you contact us about an accessibility problem and are not satisfied with our response,
please tell us: we would rather hear it directly.

## Review

We will review this statement at least once a year, and after any significant change to the
