Who We Are

The WUFPA Army

From 14 pioneers in 2017 to a regional cinematic force of over 300 production houses.

300+Production Houses
6Sub-Regions Covered
2017Year Founded

WUFPA is a legally registered, non-profit organization limited by guarantee. Headquartered in Mbarara, we unite film producers across Ankole (Rwizi), Kigezi, Rwenzori, Tooro, Bunyoro, and Greater Bushenyi.

Our mission is to nurture visionary filmmakers capable of writing, directing, producing, and acting in socially impactful and culturally rooted films. We use cinema as a tool for education, cultural tourism, and community development.

  • Capacity Building & UCC Masterclasses
  • Fighting Film Piracy & Copyright Infringement
  • The WUFPA Kibanda Initiative (Mobile Cinema)
  • Western Uganda Filmmakers SACCO (Financial Inclusion)
  • International Festival Exposure (ZIFF, Mashariki)
The Visionaries

Founders & Leadership

Meet the men who turned a dream of 14 people into an army of 300+ filmmakers. Click to read their full stories.

Katabazi George

President (2022–2030)

CEO – Ankole City Filmz. Mr. Katabazi has been a key figure since WUFPA's inception. Under his leadership, the association expanded from 14 members to 300+ across all six sub-regions.

He pioneered the UCC Regional Film Competition (now in its 3rd edition), launched the WUFPA Kibanda Initiative, formed the Film Distributors Group, and initiated the WUFM SACCO for financial support. He has led delegations to international festivals like ZIFF and African Mashariki.

Rev. Mwesigwa Newtown Samson

Vice President (2025–2030)

CEO – UPSKY FILM NETWORK. A community-focused filmmaker and cultural leader from the Rwenzori sub-region. He produces socially conscious, educational, and culturally reflective films.

As VP, he is heavily involved in scaling up the WUFPA Kibanda Initiative (mobile community screenings), planning the inaugural WUFPA Film Awards Gala, and strengthening grassroots filmmaker engagement in Western Uganda.

Mr. Cyril Baryabawe

Founder & 1st President (2017–2022)

CEO – Kigezi Universal Drama Actors/Artist, Kabale. A visionary founder who led the registration of WUFPA with URSB in 2017 alongside 14 pioneering producers.

His early leadership focused on building unity and raising awareness on film copyright. He led bold campaigns emphasizing that infringement on one’s copyright is a crime—a vital stance when piracy was normalized in the region.

Mr. Mushana Amos

Founding Vice President (2017–2022)

CEO – Banyankitara Films. A key member of the founding team who helped mobilize the original 14 producers and shaped the association's early structure and vision.

Through Banyankitara Films, he has been instrumental in promoting local language film content, ensuring that the identity, dialect, and culture of the Banyankitara people are preserved on screen for future generations.
